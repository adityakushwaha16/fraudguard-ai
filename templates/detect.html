Analyze a transaction

Enter only what is known before the transaction executes. Balances after the transaction are deliberately not requested: they are an outcome of the transaction and would leak the answer. Only TRANSFER and CASH_OUT are supported (the only PaySim types that contain fraud).

PaySim uses hourly steps; hour of day = step mod 24.

The example buttons only pre-fill form fields. The result always comes from the trained model.

What you will get

  • Prediction: Fraudulent or Legitimate
  • Fraud probability and Low/Medium/High risk
  • SHAP-based reasons for the score
  • The result is saved to your history
